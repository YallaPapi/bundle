How to read these numbers, updates & storage

Why account figures can be larger than video plays: The account source includes content outside our tracked-video list and always covers the last 30 days. Our video numbers cover the videos we know about. These are different sets of content and dates. Bundle also currently sends identical account reach and content-shown values, so those two fields are flagged rather than presented as independently verified people or views.

What all time means here: The newest cumulative counts for all tracked videos, each counted once. It is not every Instagram post ever made. The latest number replaces the previous number; we never add overlapping readings or overlapping 30-day totals.

Dates and missing data: Rankings use videos first published during the selected dates, with their total counts so far. Activity charts estimate changes between dated readings on all tracked videos. Missing readings are unknown, not zero, and today is incomplete. Current followers and account-wide 30-day figures keep their own stated periods.

Bundle refreshes analytics about once every 24 hours. This dashboard reads the retained history once a day and catches up after downtime, within Bundle’s 30-day retention. Sync analytics reads existing snapshots; it does not force a platform refresh.

Video plays are lifetime post totals. Daily gains are estimates from consecutive daily snapshots; corrections or gaps longer than 36 hours are left unknown. Net follower growth compares the same account on consecutive observation dates. A sum of account followers does not represent unique people across the network.

Account history and daily summaries stay in the local database. Detailed post snapshots are kept for 90 days plus one older baseline per post. No videos are stored. Back up the database file to protect your saved history.
