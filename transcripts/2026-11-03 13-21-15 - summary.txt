Summary: VA cookie-video production

The five VAs will shift from creating accounts and posting memes to filming cookie-baking content. Each person will be responsible for one recipe. The stated goal is enough footage for 50 finished videos per recipe per day: approximately 250 videos daily across five recipes.

What the VAs need to do

- Follow a supplied reference video and recipe, or suggest an easy-to-film chocolate-chip cookie recipe. Break the reference into scenes at its cuts. The example in the recording has approximately 10 scenes. [01:18–04:30]

- Film enough varied footage for roughly 50 usable versions of each required scene. Change the camera angle or position and the bowl's position. Examples include showing finished cookies, pulling one apart, mixing batter, adding flour or chocolate chips, scooping dough and placing it on a tray. [01:56–07:54]

- For repeatable actions, record longer continuous clips, such as about a minute of mixing. Pour ingredients slowly where practical to get more usable footage. The central editing process can split these longer recordings into short clips. [05:10–06:33]

- Send raw footage rather than assembled finished videos. Faces, narration and on-screen text are not required. Organize footage in numbered scene folders, such as "Clip 1", "Clip 2" and "Clip 3 - long clip". [08:39–10:15]

- Trim individual takes in CapCut so they contain only the requested action. Remove dead time and the moments when an action is being reset, such as returning a dough scoop to the bowl. [10:19–11:06]

- Working together is allowed, while each VA remains responsible for a recipe. Several phones can capture different angles and reduce the time required. An oven or air fryer can be used; match the reference as closely as practical. [03:41–04:01; 12:14–14:37]

- Ingredient costs will be covered. Buying an oven may be considered later, depending on how the work goes. [13:17–13:43]

- Deliver bright, clear footage with a clean camera lens and no distracting TV noise. Take the work seriously and apply corrections after feedback. Ask Joji for clarification about the requested shot variations. [13:55–15:31]

- The start date is not confirmed in the recording. Anyone who does not want to switch to this work should say so. [11:39–11:55; 15:37–15:55]

Important clarification

The speaker sometimes calls the target "50 segments", but later explicitly states that the objective is enough content for 50 complete videos per recipe per day. That means enough versions of EVERY required scene, not just 50 short clips total. Long recordings can supply multiple usable versions. [13:43–14:21]
