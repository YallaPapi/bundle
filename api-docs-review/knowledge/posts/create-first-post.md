# Make your first posts with bundle.social

> Create and schedule your first post across several platforms at once, with per-platform customization and the Discord and Slack quirk explained.

<Warning>
  🥺 YOU CAN POST DIFFERENT CONTENT TO ALL THE PLATFORMS ALL AT ONCE 🥴
</Warning>

In this tutorial you will create, customize, and publish your first post across several platforms at once.

## Open the post creator

On the sidebar, click **Posts**.

  <img src="https://mintcdn.com/bundlesocial/Xo58NLADtmHCNVc_/images/knowledge/posts/sidebar-posts.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=Xo58NLADtmHCNVc_&q=85&s=603ef6ab9e696941e2ec2b9bcb7334a9" width="482" height="456" data-path="images/knowledge/posts/sidebar-posts.png" />
</Frame>

This is your **Posts** dashboard. It's empty for now - click the **+** icon to open the post creator.

<Frame type="glass">
  <img src="https://mintcdn.com/bundlesocial/Xo58NLADtmHCNVc_/images/knowledge/posts/posts-dashboard-empty.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=Xo58NLADtmHCNVc_&q=85&s=6cad99b107810142968e1b57a0d38af6" width="1053" height="495" data-path="images/knowledge/posts/posts-dashboard-empty.png" />

The first page of the post creator is strictly internal. Here you pick which account to post on, what content to post, and when.

<Frame type="glass">
  <img src="https://mintcdn.com/bundlesocial/Xo58NLADtmHCNVc_/images/knowledge/posts/post-creator.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=Xo58NLADtmHCNVc_&q=85&s=f8b63488115fd37ce013e4044a79764b" width="800" height="655" data-path="images/knowledge/posts/post-creator.png" />
</Frame>

<Note>
  You can change all of this later, so if your creative juices aren't flowing
  from the get-go, don't worry.
</Note>

## Pick the platforms

At the top of the screen you'll see every account connected **to the team you're currently on**. Click a tile to select the account you want to post on.

<Frame type="glass">
  <img src="https://mintcdn.com/bundlesocial/Xo58NLADtmHCNVc_/images/knowledge/posts/select-account.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=Xo58NLADtmHCNVc_&q=85&s=aba2de903fef4171f73b2741714b22ac" width="1021" height="768" data-path="images/knowledge/posts/select-account.png" />
</Frame>

For this tutorial we'll post to all four connected platforms:

* **YouTube** and **Instagram** for video
* **LinkedIn** for company updates
* **Discord** to engage with the community

## Pick the content

You might be on the go and not have the right content on hand. That's why we have the **media library** - internal storage for all of your assets. [Learn more about it here.](/knowledge/posts/media-library)

Click **Select media**.

<Frame type="glass">
  <img src="https://mintcdn.com/bundlesocial/Xo58NLADtmHCNVc_/images/knowledge/posts/select-media-button.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=Xo58NLADtmHCNVc_&q=85&s=1626d0332a1c57e7c98c4675e842866e" width="1028" height="745" data-path="images/knowledge/posts/select-media-button.png" />
</Frame>

Here you can see all of your media. Pick from what's there or **upload something new**. We'll add one new photo and pull the rest from the [media library](/knowledge/posts/media-library).

<Frame type="glass">
  <img src="https://mintcdn.com/bundlesocial/Xo58NLADtmHCNVc_/images/knowledge/posts/media-library.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=Xo58NLADtmHCNVc_&q=85&s=e4a86aba032eb2741eeaf34b0198df5a" width="934" height="670" data-path="images/knowledge/posts/media-library.png" />
</Frame>

## Set the time

Say your boss wants a post every 3 hours. Instead of sitting down every 3 hours, pick a time once and forget about it.

<Frame type="glass">
  <img src="https://mintcdn.com/bundlesocial/Xo58NLADtmHCNVc_/images/knowledge/posts/set-time.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=Xo58NLADtmHCNVc_&q=85&s=1ef427542db35a722b4bb94a28f292f7" width="1032" height="838" data-path="images/knowledge/posts/set-time.png" />
</Frame>

<Tip>
  If you don't set a time, or set one earlier than the current time, the post
  publishes as soon as processing finishes.
</Tip>

### Adjust the content per platform

Each tab has its own content field. Hover over a piece of content to delete it - for example, on the YouTube tab we keep only the video we want to post.

<Frame type="glass">
  <img src="https://mintcdn.com/bundlesocial/Xo58NLADtmHCNVc_/images/knowledge/posts/adjust-content-youtube.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=Xo58NLADtmHCNVc_&q=85&s=3b7cd43204c0311fe8503c947d712070" width="810" height="829" data-path="images/knowledge/posts/adjust-content-youtube.png" />
</Frame>

<Warning>
  To post multiple images - for example a carousel on Instagram - just leave the
  content untouched. We support multi-content on every platform that offers it.
</Warning>

Next, switch to the Instagram tab and remove any content you don't want there.

<Info>You can also add content at this step.</Info>

  <img src="https://mintcdn.com/bundlesocial/Xo58NLADtmHCNVc_/images/knowledge/posts/adjust-content-instagram.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=Xo58NLADtmHCNVc_&q=85&s=f23485cd43ffbf21c6e047422e831540" width="894" height="626" data-path="images/knowledge/posts/adjust-content-instagram.png" />

## Discord and Slack quirk

If you post to Discord or Slack without changing the sender, your message shows up like this:

<Card>
  <Frame type="glass">
    <img src="https://mintcdn.com/bundlesocial/Xo58NLADtmHCNVc_/images/knowledge/posts/discord-default-sender.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=Xo58NLADtmHCNVc_&q=85&s=eaa40940371f44a4dda5e995a8fc3e9b" width="201" height="57" data-path="images/knowledge/posts/discord-default-sender.png" />
  </Frame>
</Card>

That's fine if you don't mind. If you do, click the **+** icon to create a new bot.

<Card>
  <Frame type="glass">
    <img src="https://mintcdn.com/bundlesocial/Xo58NLADtmHCNVc_/images/knowledge/posts/create-bot-button.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=Xo58NLADtmHCNVc_&q=85&s=193db0bd24a6b35f5d49aced4eb988b8" width="992" height="637" data-path="images/knowledge/posts/create-bot-button.png" />
  </Frame>
</Card>

A popup appears - upload a bot cover photo and give it a name. The name is shown as the sender in Discord/Slack, so choose carefully. Then click **Create**.

<Card>
  <Frame type="glass">
    <img src="https://mintcdn.com/bundlesocial/Xo58NLADtmHCNVc_/images/knowledge/posts/create-bot-modal.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=Xo58NLADtmHCNVc_&q=85&s=be794a55569db9131738bbac293ed2f2" width="472" height="451" data-path="images/knowledge/posts/create-bot-modal.png" />
  </Frame>
</Card>

Your bot is ready to reuse next time. **Don't forget to select the newly created bot from the dropdown.**

## Publish

With your captions and images in place, it's time to post. Click **Publish**.

<Frame type="glass">
  <img src="https://mintcdn.com/bundlesocial/Xo58NLADtmHCNVc_/images/knowledge/posts/publish-button.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=Xo58NLADtmHCNVc_&q=85&s=9a951ba32b9cad5cefde39c11c60c7b1" width="278" height="60" data-path="images/knowledge/posts/publish-button.png" />
</Frame>

The page refreshes and you're back on the **Posts** dashboard - this time with your post on it. If it includes a lot of content, like several large videos, the post may show a **processing** or **scheduled** status. Don't worry, the rest will be published.

<Frame type="glass">
  <img src="https://mintcdn.com/bundlesocial/Xo58NLADtmHCNVc_/images/knowledge/posts/post-dashboard.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=Xo58NLADtmHCNVc_&q=85&s=be96ed94bab7b2e526f20c08a66f7ee7" width="900" height="426" data-path="images/knowledge/posts/post-dashboard.png" />
</Frame>

You can also switch to the **calendar view** to see your scheduled and published posts laid out by date.

<Frame type="glass">
