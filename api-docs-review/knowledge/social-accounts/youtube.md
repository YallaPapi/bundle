# Connect your Youtube channel to bundle.social

> Complete guide to connecting your YouTube channel to bundle.social. Learn how to authorize permissions and start scheduling YouTube videos.

In this tutorial, you will learn how to connect your YouTube channel to your team.

<h2>First things first, create a team or be a part of one</h2>
If you don't know what the teams are, [click here](/knowledge/teams/what-are-teams)
to get up to speed.

## Connect your YouTube channel

bundle.social is built in a way that won't let you down. If you are in a team and
there is no social account connected, your dashboard will look like this.

## Errors

There should be no errors at this point, but if there are, it will be these two:

<Warning>Error connecting to YouTube</Warning>
This can mean many things outside our control, maybe you don't have a YouTube channel
connected to this Google account or YouTube had a bug or there was a timeout. If
you get this error, simply retry. Most often, it will help.

<Warning>
  You didn't grant all the necessary permissions
</Warning>

This means that you didn't grant all the permissions we need to post on your behalf. You can try to reconnect and grant all the permissions.

<Warning>
  You don't have enough permissions on any of your YouTube channels.
</Warning>

Remember when we earlier told you to grant the app all access? You probably didn't. <br />
Click **Disconnect** and connect your account again, this time with all
permissions.
