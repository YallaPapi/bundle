# Connect your Linkedin account to bundle.social

> Tutorial on connecting your LinkedIn account or page to bundle.social. Learn how to authorize permissions and start scheduling LinkedIn posts.

In this short tutorial you will learn how to connect your Linkedin account to bundle.social

<h2>First things first, create a team or be a part of one</h2>
If you don't know what the teams are, [click here](/knowledge/teams/what-are-teams)
to get up to speed.

## Connect your Linkedin account

bundle.social is built in a way that won't let you down. If you are in a team and
there is no social account connected, your dashboard will look like this.

## Errors

There should be no errors at this point, but if there are, it will be these two:

<Warning>Error connecting to Linkedin</Warning>
This can mean many things outside our control, maybe you don't have a Linkedin account
connected to this Google account or Linkedin had a bug or there was a timeout. If
you get this error, simply retry. Most often, it will help.

<Warning>
  You didn't grant all the necessary permissions
</Warning>

This means that you didn't grant all the permissions we need to post on your behalf. You can try to reconnect and grant all the permissions.

<Warning>
  You don't have enough permissions in any of your LinkedIn organizations.
</Warning>

You are probably not an admin for picked account.
