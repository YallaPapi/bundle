# Connect your Facebook account to bundle.social

> Complete tutorial on connecting your Facebook page to bundle.social. Learn how to authorize permissions and start scheduling Facebook posts.

In this tutorial, you will learn how to connect your Facebook account to your team.

<h2>First things first, create a team or be a part of one</h2>
If you don't know what the teams are, [click here](/knowledge/teams/what-are-teams)
to get up to speed.

## Connect your Facebook account

<Note>
  Meta sometimes asks whether to use **Business** login or a **standard** connection. If a **Page does not appear** in the picker, reconnect and try the **other** option-many accounts only show up with business-scoped permissions. Same story if our app shows **Reauthenticate**: use it when the connection looks stale instead of assuming the picker is broken. More detail: [Connect Social Accounts](/api-reference/connect-social-accounts) (section **withBusinessScope**).
</Note>

## Errors

There should be no errors at this point, but if there are, they will likely be one of these two:

<Warning>Error connecting Facebook.</Warning>
This can mean many things outside our control, maybe Facebook had a bug or there
was a timeout. If you get this error, simply retry. Most often, it will help.

<Warning>
  You didn't grant all the necessary permissions
</Warning>

This means that you didn't grant all the permissions we need to post on your behalf. You can try to reconnect and grant all the permissions.

<Warning>
  You don't have enough permissions on any of your Facebook pages.
</Warning>

You are probably not an admin for your Facebook page.
